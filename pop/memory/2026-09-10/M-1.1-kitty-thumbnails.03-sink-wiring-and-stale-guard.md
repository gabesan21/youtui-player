# Sink wiring and stale-thumbnail guard

`CustomList` and the player box pick the sink at construction: blank Kitty placeholder box vs the unchanged `tview.Image` blocks path. All six thumbnail fetch sites were unified into `fetchListThumbnail`, and `SetThumbnail`/`SetKittyThumbnail` only paint when the item's current track URL still matches the fetched one — fixing the pre-existing stale-page race together with stale placements. The player box fetch is guarded by `thumbnailFetchStale` (`mu`-guarded `isPlaying`/`currentThumb`).
